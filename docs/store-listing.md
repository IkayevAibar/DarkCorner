## English (default)

**App name** (30 characters at most):

> Dark Corner

**Short description** (80 at most):

> Ten dark Floors, one Ancient Dragon. A solo dungeon RPG: offline, no ads.

**Full description** (4000 at most):

> Dark Corner is a dark-fantasy dungeon RPG you play alone, at your own pace. No internet, no ads, no account.
>
> Make a Hero from twelve Classes: Fighter, Rogue, Wizard, Cleric, Barbarian, Ranger, Paladin, Warlock, Monk, Druid, Bard or Sorcerer. Then take them down through ten Floors of the Labyrinth to the Ancient Dragon.
>
> THE LABYRINTH
> • Every Door gives a Clue to what waits behind it, and shows its Threat before you choose.
> • Fight on Auto, or turn by turn: pick a Stance, Dodge, Help your Companion, or try an Escape roll.
> • Treasure rooms, riddles, cursed altars, locked caches, Hidden rooms and Mini-bosses.
> • Waypoints, Camps and Town Portals take you back where you left off.
>
> DAYS, NOT TIMERS
> • Time stands still while you play. A Day ends when your Hero sleeps: at the Tavern, in a Camp, or rough on the stones.
> • Each morning brings new Bounties, a new Omen, fresh gear in the Shop, and the Well's Daily Delve.
>
> A COMPANION
> • Hire a Hero of another Class at the Tavern. It fights beside you, opens Twin doors with you and shares Duo Chests.
> • Keep its Loyalty: share at the Oathstones, pay its wage each morning, and dress it from your Bag.
>
> GEAR WORTH KEEPING
> • Items in seven Tiers, from Common to Relic, many found unidentified.
> • Upgrade, reforge and salvage at the Forge. Win Titles for your Deeds.
>
> A CHAPTER WITH AN END
> • The Boss gate opens when you first reach Floor 10.
> • Slay the Dragon to end the Chapter and enter your own Hall of Fame.
>
> YOUR GAME, YOUR RULES
> • Story, Normal or Hard.
> • Iron mode: one life. A fallen Hero is gone for good.
> • Save your game to a file to keep it safe or move it to a new phone.
>
> In English and Russian. Rules material from the System Reference Document 5.2, under CC BY 4.0; full credits in the game.

## The forms in Play Console (App content)

- **Privacy policy:** the URL above.
- **Ads:** no ads.
- **App access:** all of it works without signing in.
- **Content rating (IARC):** a game.
  - Violence: fantasy violence against monsters, with no blood or gore shown.
  - Gambling: answer yes to simulated gambling. The Goblin gambler's dice take bets in game gold, with no real money and no prizes. This may raise the rating.
  - No user-made content, no chat, no location sharing, no purchases.
- **Target audience:** 13 and older, so the Families policy doesn't apply. Don't tick under 13.
- **Data safety:**
  - Does the app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.**
  - The app has no network permission, so nothing is sent off the device.
- **News app:** no. **Government app:** no. **Financial features:** none. **Health:** none.
- **EU Digital Services Act:** not a trader. The game is free, with no ads or purchases.

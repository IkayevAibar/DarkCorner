## Review at phone size

All 27 selected maps were visually inspected at 375 CSS px, at 78% Room-view brightness and 62% fight brightness with sample tokens. The first source in each theme was also inspected in all eight rotations/mirrors. The standalone HTML offers these controls for every source; it reads the existing approved maps and tokens from the checkout.

These are composition previews, not screenshots of the running game. Browser validation decoded every displayed image across 12 captures, verified 375 × 375 CSS px map frames, and found no page errors or horizontal overflow at a 375 × 812 viewport.

The three existing maps appear beside the new goblin, crypt and demon sources in the review sheets. The lair receives its first dedicated set. All selected maps have four visible midpoint openings and a broad central floor. The lava Room carries the strongest color accent; its glow remains along the wall, while the token area stays subdued.

### Refinements and second passes

- `goblins-3.png`: added stone crossings at the top and right doorway approaches.
- `demons-2.png`: moved the altar into the upper-left corner and cleared the bottom threshold.
- `demons-4.png`: removed a small generated signature-like mark at the bottom-left edge.

The original versions are preserved under [drafts/](drafts/), and the exact edit prompts are recorded in `generation-prompts.json`. No further source-art second pass is identified. Final integration still needs review after delivery compression and real encounter layouts.
